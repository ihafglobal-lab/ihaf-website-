Who we are

A shared mission, stronger together.

Humanitarian challenges are too large for any one organisation to solve alone. IHAF’s vision is to help charities, aid organisations, donors, volunteers and communities cooperate so support reaches people more effectively.

With roots intended in Uganda and a global outlook, we aim for partnerships that put people first.

Our guiding commitments

✓Humanity — reduce suffering wherever people need help.
✓Impartiality — support based on need, without discrimination.
✓Integrity — honest stewardship and accountability.
✓Cooperation — respect partners and work through voluntary agreements.
✓Local leadership — listen to communities and support local solutions.

How we aim to work

From good intentions to practical action

Trust is built through clear responsibilities, community participation and results that can be explained.

01 Listen to communities and understand the need.
02 Work with credible local and international partners.

04 Review outcomes, learn and improve the response.
